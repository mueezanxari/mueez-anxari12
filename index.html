I manage digital content end-to-end — video, website and social — scheduling and publishing through Meta Business Suite, designing carousels and reels in Canva, and writing SEO copy (including alt text) that's built around what audiences are actually searching for, using Google Analytics to guide it.

Growth, for me, is proof-driven.
